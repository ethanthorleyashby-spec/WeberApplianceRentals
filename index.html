Questions

The short answers. Full details are in the Rental Terms.

What do I need in my home?

A washer needs hot and cold water hookups and a drain. A dryer needs a dryer outlet (3-prong or 4-prong) or a gas line, plus a vent. You'll tell us which you have when you sign up so we bring the right cord.

When am I charged?

Your first month is charged when you sign up, then on the same day each month after that. Payments are automatic.

What if a machine breaks?

Text us. Repairs and replacements are included at no cost, unless the damage was caused by misuse or neglect.

How do I cancel?

Give us 4 weeks' notice by text or email. You'll keep paying until your notice period ends, then we pick up the machines. If you need a pickup with less than 4 weeks' notice, there's a $50 short-notice fee.

What if my card payment fails?

We'll automatically retry your card and email you. If the payment is still unpaid 2 weeks after it was due, a $20 late fee applies and we may pick up the machines.

Is there a deposit or delivery fee?

No. Delivery and setup are free.

I'm moving. Can I take the machines?

Text us before you move. If your new place is in our area, we can usually move them for you. Please don't move them yourself.
